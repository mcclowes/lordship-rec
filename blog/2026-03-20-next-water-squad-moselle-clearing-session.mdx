The next river cleanup is scheduled for Saturday 21st March, 10:00am to approximately 1:00pm.

Recent heavy rainfall has deposited significant debris on the outflow grille, which will be the primary focus. Depending on conditions, we may start work between the second and third bridges on our project to create a mini wetland and backwaters on the vegetated area that extends from the south bank.

**Meeting location:** Gather at the outflow grille near the Broadwater Farm end of the river.

**To participate:** Contact John Miles at johnmiles68@yahoo.co.uk to confirm your attendance and help organise necessary tools and equipment.

**What to bring:**

- Weather-appropriate clothing
- Sturdy footwear
- Protective clothing for muddy work and potential scratches
- Commitment to safe working practices and good hygiene standards
