The Friends of Lordship Rec invite all park users to an open meeting on Sunday 5th October at 2pm at Lordship Hub (N17 6NU).

{/* truncate */}

## What to expect

Attendees can expect discussions about recent developments affecting the recreation ground, community events, and opportunities to volunteer in maintenance and improvement efforts. The meeting provides an accessible entry point for those interested in becoming involved with park stewardship.

## Volunteering information

For those curious about volunteering opportunities, organisers have created a survey form at [https://www.surveymonkey.co.uk/r/KF97ZXC](https://www.surveymonkey.co.uk/r/KF97ZXC) where interested parties can provide their contact information and learn more about available roles.
