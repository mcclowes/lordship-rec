To mark the 25th anniversary of the Friends organisation, which was established at a June 2001 gathering of 50 park users, we have produced a promotional leaflet. This is the inaugural printed material of its kind for Lordship Rec.

{/* truncate */}

We have produced 3,000 copies of the flyer, which highlight the park's amenities, recreational spaces, community groups, and historical significance. Copies are available for pickup at the Hub information desk.
