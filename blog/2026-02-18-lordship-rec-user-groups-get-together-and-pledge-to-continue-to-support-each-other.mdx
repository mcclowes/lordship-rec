The community groups operating within and around Lordship Rec convened on February 17th for a significant gathering. During this meeting, representatives shared updates and made collective commitments.

{/* truncate */}

## Key pledges

The groups committed to:

- Continuing mutual support among organisations
- Celebrating a quarter-century of collaborative effort to restore and improve the recreation ground
- Maintaining partnership with the Council on planned enhancements
- Opposing any inappropriate commercialisation in the Rec in the future

The full meeting minutes are available in the attached PDF document for those seeking additional details about the discussion and outcomes.
