The Friends of Lordship Rec are seeking donations to raise £1,000 for essential operating expenses. As stated in our appeal, most of the external funding we obtain can only be spent on specific projects.

{/* truncate */}

### Current financial situation

The organisation faces increased costs this year, particularly a 30% rise in public liability insurance premiums to £630, which will deplete our reserves. Limited financial reserves in 2025 prevented implementation of several beneficial initiatives.

### How donations help

Funds support:

- Public liability insurance for volunteering activities
- Tool repair and replacement
- Community events and organised volunteer work
- Maintenance of green areas (woodland, orchard, grasslands)
- Management of blue areas (lake and Moselle channel)

### About the Friends

Operating since 2001 as an autonomous community organisation, the Friends have transformed Lordship Rec from the neglected and underused public park it was 25 years ago into a vibrant community space. We work alongside Haringey Council and coordinate with other local groups on maintenance and facility improvements.
