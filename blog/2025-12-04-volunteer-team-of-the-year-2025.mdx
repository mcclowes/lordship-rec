The Friends of Lordship Rec have been named Volunteer Team of the Year 2025.

## Recognition at the ceremony

The Friends of Lordship Rec received the following commendation:

"Friends of Lordship Rec have been volunteering in Lordship Rec for 24 years. They now co-manage the park with the Council. They have transformed the park from neglected wilderness to a thriving popular park, with great facilities and huge areas of beautiful nature and biodiversity."

The organisation has engaged hundreds of volunteers in ongoing activities including litter collection, river and lake maintenance, fundraising for park improvements, community events, and nature walks. Their efforts have raised substantial funds for enhancing park amenities and programming for residents.
