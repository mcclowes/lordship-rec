## Overview

The Shell Theatre Performing Arts Collective was established in 2012 to coordinate performances and manage the refurbished open-air theatre throughout the year.

## Founding Meeting (April 17, 2012)

The inaugural meeting brought together representatives from the [Friends of Lordship Rec](/about), Wildcat Arts Collective, Big Dance, Tottenham Community Choir, Haringey Music Centre, and local residents.

Key discussion points included:

- Risk assessment and insurance requirements
- Electricity funding and availability
- Key access procedures
- Fundraising strategies
- Management structure for the venue

The group emphasised creating "a regular programme of events, especially in the Summer, so that local people could see its potential" and felt it essential that "local people felt it was for them and run by them."

## Early Programs

The collective aimed for the Shell to serve local performers and connect with area performing arts groups and youth centres. Since its inception, major events have included:

- One World Folk Festival (international music and dance)
- Jazz Fest (swing bands and lounge jazz)
- Blockorama (steel pan performances)

The group's vision centred on making the Shell Theatre an accessible community venue reflecting local artistic talent.
