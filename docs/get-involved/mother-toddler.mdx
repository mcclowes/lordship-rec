## Overview

The Mother & Toddler Group operates a casual drop-in programme for parents and young children at the Hub facility. Sessions are held twice weekly on Tuesdays and Thursdays, running from 11am each day.

## Details

Participation costs £2 per child, with "a lunchtime snack" included in the fee.
