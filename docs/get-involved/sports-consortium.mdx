## Purpose

This voluntary, community organisation delivers sports and creative activities by working collaboratively to facilitate a year-round programme of participatory offerings at Lordship Rec.

## Aims

The group focuses on understanding local perspectives and involving community members in planning. They investigate funding opportunities, engage diverse sections of the community, and encourage park users to organise public social activities while fostering mutual appreciation and respect. Their work addresses health inequalities.

## Key Objectives

- Provide strategic direction for physical, creative, and wellbeing activities
- Reduce inequalities in activity access across Lordship Rec and surrounding areas
- Commission high-quality services meeting community needs
- Employ evidence-based approaches addressing health disparities
- Coordinate key service providers
- Support voluntary and community organisations in expanding participation
- Highlight benefits of physical and creative engagement
- Enhance activity quality within the community
- Deliver training and education opportunities
