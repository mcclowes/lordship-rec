The Friends of Harmony Gardens maintain the Harmony Gardens, a community growing project next to Broadwater Farm Community Centre. Volunteers welcome to help with gardening and DIY.

Contact: [rutharooot@blueyonder.co.uk](mailto:rutharooot@blueyonder.co.uk)

(best days to visit on site or volunteer are Tuesday and Friday afternoons and some Sundays)
