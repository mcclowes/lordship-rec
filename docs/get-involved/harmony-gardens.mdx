## Harmony Gardens

The Friends of Harmony Gardens manage a community gardening initiative positioned adjacent to Broadwater Farm Community Centre. This volunteer-driven growing project welcomes individuals interested in participating in both gardening and DIY activities.

**Contact:** [rutharooot@blueyonder.co.uk](mailto:rutharooot@blueyonder.co.uk)

**Best times to visit or volunteer:** Tuesday and Friday afternoons, plus select Sundays
