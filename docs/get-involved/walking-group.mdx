There are a range of walking groups with weekly open walks. As of 2022 this included Silverfit, Buggy Walk, Wheelytots, the Monday walk (see below), and the Kurdish/Turkish Women's Group…

One of them meets every Monday morning at the Downhills Park Road entrance, 9.20am for a brisk healthy walk around the Rec. They've been doing it since the mid-2000s! Why not join them, or one of the others…
