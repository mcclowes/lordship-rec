## Overview

Lordship Recreation Ground hosts several weekly walking groups that are open to the public. As of 2022, these include Silverfit, Buggy Walk, Wheelytots, the Monday walk, and the Kurdish/Turkish Women's Group.

## Monday Morning Walk

One established group meets every Monday morning at the Downhills Park Road entrance at 9:20am for a brisk healthy walk around the Rec. They've been doing it since the mid-2000s. New participants are encouraged to join this or any of the other available walking groups.
