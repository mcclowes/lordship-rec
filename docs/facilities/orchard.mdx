The orchard was established in 2012 when the park was regenerated. Since then groups of volunteers have maintained and added to it. They have organised workshops on tree and meadow care and held annual public events like apple day and Wassail.

If you would like to get involved, please contact: [shaywill@fastmail.co.uk](mailto:shaywill@fastmail.co.uk)
