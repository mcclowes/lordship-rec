In September 2005, a festival marked the opening of three new play facilities in Lordship Rec:

- **A freestyle skateboard/BMX area**
- **A multi-use games area** for football and basketball which was recently refurbished.

**The Broadwater Community Centre** is a base for youth football teams who use the field in the Rec for matches.
