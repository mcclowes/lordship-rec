Lordship Rec has several areas of interest to both young and old. The very young can play in the playground, paddle in the paddling pool in the summer months or ride their bikes around the model traffic area. The older children can play football on the grass or play ball games in the multi-use games area or they can take their skate boards or bmx bikes to the skate board area or BMX dirt track. For those over 16 there is the new outdoor gym next to the Hub and lake for the whole body workout! If you just like to sit an enjoy nature then there is the lake with picnic tables and benches and the wooded areas and the river or if you like to keep moving there is lots of green space to walk around in or jog through or to take your dog for a walk in. Not to mention the Hub with its Café!
