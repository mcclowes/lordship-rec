The Friends organisation carries out substantial volunteer work throughout the park, including regular conservation and litter removal initiatives. Their maintenance responsibilities span multiple areas: the Woodland and Spinney (supported by TCV — The Conservation Volunteers), the River (in partnership with Thames 21 and the Haringey Water Squad), the lake, orchard, and meadows. The [Friends of Harmony Gardens](/get-involved/harmony-gardens) separately manage the community garden space.

## Lordship Rec Management Plan 2015–2025

A comprehensive planning document governs park operations over the next decade. This collaborative strategy was developed jointly by user groups and Haringey Council, detailing all park features, their upkeep requirements, and management approaches. The document outlines the partnership structure between community groups and the Council, coordinated through the [Lordship Rec Users Forum](/get-involved/users-forum).

As noted in the plan: "It explains all the features in the park, how they are to be maintained, and how they are being managed in partnership."

The plan includes appendices with additional information and catalogs current user groups and their activities, plus lists of proposed future enhancements.
