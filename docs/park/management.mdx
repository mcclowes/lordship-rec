The Friends undertake substantial volunteer work in the park, conducting regular conservation and litterpicking activities. They maintain the Woodland and Spinney with support from TCV (The Conservation Volunteers), the River alongside Thames 21 and the Haringey Water Squad, the lake, the orchard and the meadows. The [Friends of Harmony Gardens](/get-involved/harmony-gardens) care for the community garden.

## Lordship Rec Management Plan 2015–2025

Posted on 24th June 2015 by Dave Morris.

[www.haringey.gov.uk/sites/haringeygovuk/files/lordship_rec_2015_-_final.pdf](http://www.haringey.gov.uk/sites/haringeygovuk/files/lordship_rec_2015_-_final.pdf)

This important document outlines "the updated Plan for Lordship Rec, applying for the next 10 years, written collaboratively and agreed by the park's user groups and by the Council." It details park features, maintenance approaches, and management partnerships coordinated through the [Lordship Rec Users Forum](/get-involved/users-forum). The plan documents user groups, their activities, further improvements sought, and includes useful appendices with additional details.
