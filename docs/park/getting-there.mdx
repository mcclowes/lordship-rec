Lordship Rec is positioned within Tottenham's boundaries, bordered by Lordship Lane, Walpole Road, Higham Road, Adams Road, and Downhills Park Road.

## Nearest public transport

**Tubes and trains:**

- Wood Green and Turnpike Lane (Piccadilly Line)
- Seven Sisters (Victoria Line)
- Bruce Grove (Overground)

All stations are approximately 15–20 minutes' walk from the park.

## Bus routes

- **243**: Services between Wood Green Tube and Seven Sisters, stopping at Lordship Lane Gate
- **123**: Connects Wood Green Tube and Turnpike Lane with Seven Sisters, accessing Lordship Lane Gate
- **W4**: Runs from Turnpike Lane, Wood Green, or Tottenham High Road to Higham Road or Downhills Park Road Gates
