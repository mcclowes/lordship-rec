---

The Rec is bordered by Lordship Lane, Walpole Road, Higham Road, Adams Road and Downhills Park Road in Tottenham.

**The nearest tubes and trains**:

- Wood Green and Turnpike Lane (Piccadilly Line)
- Seven Sisters (Victoria Line)
- Bruce Grove overground

It is approx 15–20 min walk from all these stations.

**Buses**

- **243** can be taken going into London from Wood Green Tube or towards Wood Green from Seven Sisters to the Lordship Lane Gate of Lordship Rec.
- **123** can be taken going East from Wood Green Tube and Turnpike Lane or towards Wood Green from Seven Sisters to the Lordship Lane Gate of Lordship Rec.
- **W4** can be taken from Turnpike Lane or Wood Green or from Tottenham High Road to the Higham Road or Downhills Park Road Gates of Lordship Rec.
