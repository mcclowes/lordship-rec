Lordship Recreation Ground, known as "the Rec," is a district park in Tottenham, North London (Borough of Haringey). It serves diverse users including families, young people, walkers, joggers, cyclists, and dog walkers.

The park attracts visitors for various activities: café visits at Lordship Hub, classes, community events, playground time, nature appreciation, conservation volunteering in woodland areas, the lake, meadows, orchard, and river, plus litter-picking initiatives.

## Key features

The park is Tottenham's largest public park and offers expansive views toward Alexandra Palace. Notable attractions include:

- **Historic elements**: The Shell Theatre and the world's first [Model Traffic Area](/facilities/model-traffic-area)
- **Natural features**: The [Moselle](/facilities/river-moselle), one of London's hidden rivers, and a substantial [lake](/facilities/lake) with diverse bird populations
- **Recreation facilities**: [Young children's playground](/facilities/playground), multi-use games area, skateboard/BMX area, [dirt track for bikes](/facilities/bmx-track), and [outdoor gym](/facilities/outdoor-gym)
- **Open space**: Large field for casual games

## Cycling opportunities

The BMX dirt track and Model Traffic Area make the Rec ideal for cyclists of all ages. Bikes are available for hire at Rockstone Bike Hut, located at the Lordship Lane park entrance.
