Broadwater Farm Estate is undergoing significant refurbishment. "2 blocks have been or will soon be demolished and rebuilt and there will be new landscaping, road layout and supermarket." New housing is being constructed on the site of the former Moselle School.

The Broadwater Farm Community Centre has been returned to Council management and is currently being refurbished, with plans being developed for future community use.
