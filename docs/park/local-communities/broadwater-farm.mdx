Broadwater Farm Estate is undergoing refurbishment. Two blocks have been or will soon be demolished and rebuilt with new landscaping, road layout and supermarket. New housing is being added where the old Moselle School was located. The Broadwater Farm Community Centre has returned to Council management and is being refurbished with plans being made for its community use.
