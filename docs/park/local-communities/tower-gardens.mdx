## Tower Gardens Estate

When Lordship Recreation Ground was established during the 1930s, the Tower Gardens Estate (approximately 1,500 homes) residents served as the primary local users. The Broadwater Farm Estate, containing 1,086 homes, was constructed in 1973. Both are council-owned properties, though "right to buy" initiatives have transformed Tower Gardens Estate demographics to an estimated 40% leaseholdership, with some units now rented privately.

The majority of Tower Gardens Estate now holds Conservation Area designation. The community maintains "a long and lively history of residents' social activities and organisations, including a residents association."
