---

To book Lordship Recreation Ground, you have two options for initial inquiries:

- **Email:** [parksbookings@haringey.gov.uk](mailto:parksbookings@haringey.gov.uk)
- **Phone:** 020 8489 1418

For the full booking process and to complete your reservation, visit the official Haringey Council booking portal:

[https://www.haringey.gov.uk/libraries-sport-and-leisure/parks-and-open-spaces/events-and-activities/events-parks/booking-park](https://www.haringey.gov.uk/libraries-sport-and-leisure/parks-and-open-spaces/events-and-activities/events-parks/booking-park)
