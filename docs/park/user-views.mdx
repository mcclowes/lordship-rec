The **Lordship Rec Users Forum** was established in 2002 to unite local groups, agencies, and institutions with interests in Lordship Rec. Members include Haringey Council representatives, [The Friends of Lordship Rec](/about), local Residents Associations, and voluntary ecological or sports groups operating in the area.

The Forum convenes monthly at the Hub to discuss upcoming plans and proposals, ensuring a coordinated approach to park development. Its primary objectives are promoting communication and coordination among stakeholders and amplifying the collective voice of park users.

## Accomplishments

Since its founding, the Forum has achieved significant milestones:

- Successfully secured funding for three new play facilities
- Won Heritage Lottery Fund support for major improvements, including River Moselle restoration and Hub construction
- Created meadows and an orchard
- Refurbished historical areas like the Model Traffic Area
- Organised public consultations and developed a master plan for the park alongside a management and maintenance strategy

The Forum's vision statement details its long-term goals for the recreation ground's future development.
