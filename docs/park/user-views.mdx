**The Lordship Rec Users Forum** was established in 2002 as a collaborative group designed to unite all local organisations, agencies, and institutions with an interest in Lordship Rec. Membership comprises representatives from relevant Haringey Council departments, [The Friends of Lordship Rec](/about), local Residents Associations, and other voluntary ecological or sports groups operating in the Rec.

The Forum convenes monthly at the Hub to deliberate on plans and proposals affecting the Rec, ensuring a unified approach to future development. Its primary objectives are promoting communication and coordination while establishing itself as an influential advocate for Rec users.

Over its operational period, the Forum has achieved notable successes, including securing funding for three new play facilities and leading efforts for a Heritage Lottery Fund bid that enabled major improvements. These enhancements encompassed River Moselle restoration, Hub construction, meadow and orchard creation, and refurbishment of historical areas including the Model Traffic Area. The Forum has also organised public consultations and developed a master plan for the park alongside a management and maintenance plan.
