## Friends of Lordship Rec Policies

The organization has adopted seven core policies (as of March 2016, reviewed annually):

1. Child Protection
2. Data Protection
3. Environmental Policy
4. Equality and Diversity
5. Health and Safety
6. Volunteering
7. Financial Rules and Management

---

## Child Protection Policy

**Purpose:** FoLR is committed to protecting all children (defined as persons under 18) participating in activities from physical, financial, sexual, or emotional abuse, neglect, and bullying.

**Key Principles:**

- The welfare of the child is paramount
- All children have equal protection rights regardless of age, culture, disability, gender, language, religion, or sexual identity
- FoLR organizes activities encouraging children and parents to participate together
- Members are not expected to assume parental responsibility

**Good Practice Standards:**

- Work in open environments, avoiding private situations
- Maintain appropriate physical contact (limited to guiding movements or reassurance on arms/shoulders)
- Build relationships based on mutual trust and respect
- Do not arrange to meet children outside activities to develop friendships
- Avoid smoking or drinking alcohol around children
- Provide constructive feedback
- Keep records of injuries and treatment

**Physical Restraint:** Only used as a last resort for safety; must be reported immediately to the Child Protection Officer or Chair and recorded in the Incident Book.

## Data Protection Policy

FoLR holds information from members and mailing list subscribers for communication purposes only.

**Key Commitments:**

- "We will never sell or give your personal information to any person or any organisation"
- Members may request removal from mailing lists
- Personal information used only for expressly permitted purposes
- Sign-up sheets visible to others until transferred to email lists
- Email lists accessed only by relevant working group organizers

## Environmental Policy

FoLR minimizes environmental harm and promotes sustainability across six areas:

**1. Waste Minimization:**

- Double-sided printing, reduced font sizes
- Maximize recycling and composting

**2. Energy Reduction:**

- Use artificial lighting only when necessary
- Turn off equipment after meetings
- Avoid overfilling kettles

**3. Water Conservation:**

- Reduce water usage where possible

**4. Sustainable Land Management:**

- Eliminate artificial chemicals harmful to wildlife
- Use native plant species
- Prefer non-peat compost from local sources
- Compost weeds and clippings

**5. Responsible Purchasing:**

- Buy recycled products
- Source locally or from sustainable sources
- Avoid over-packaging

**6. Transport:**

- Encourage public transport, cycling, walking, or lift-sharing

**Awareness Raising:** FoLR promotes environmental consciousness through example and educational initiatives with park users.

## Equality and Diversity Policy

FoLR commits to creating an inclusive organization free from discrimination based on race, color, nationality, ethnicity, gender, marital status, disability, religion, economic status, age, or sexual orientation.

**Objectives:**

- Reduce, stop, and prevent discrimination
- Encourage inclusive practices and interaction styles
- Ensure all activities are open and accessible

**Definition of Inclusion:** "People feeling included means they feel a sense of belonging and feeling respected and valued for who they are."

**Implementation:**

- Officers and organizers set leading examples
- All members encouraged to avoid direct or indirect discrimination
- Concerns directed initially to the Chair; unresolved issues raised at general meetings

## Health and Safety Policy

**Purpose:** Maintain safe environments for all FoLR activities and prevent avoidable accidents.

**Responsibilities:**

*FoLR Officers:*

- Ensure adequate Public Liability insurance
- Provide appropriate information and training

*Activity Organizers:*

- Conduct risk assessments before activities begin
- Ensure activities follow appropriate procedures
- Complete and store records

*All Members:*

- Take reasonable care of themselves and others
- Report hazards to activity organizers

**Risk Assessment:** Conducted for workdays and public events (not routine meetings); generic assessments created for activity types; significant new events require separate assessments and annual reviews.

**Accidents:**

- Activity organizers manage emergencies
- First aid training signposted to active members
- First aid boxes available in Hub Office and Cafe
- All accidents recorded in the Accident Record Book

**Tools and Equipment:**

- Stored securely in Hub Plant Room and Freedom Road container
- Users responsible for safe use and return
- Lost or damaged tools reported to officers
- Organizers verify equipment safety before use

## Volunteer Policy

**Definition:** Volunteers commit time and energy freely, through personal choice, without expectation of financial reward (except expense reimbursement).

**Principles:**

- Volunteering is central to FoLR's mission
- Volunteers are essential to achieving core aims
- FoLR does not use volunteers to replace or undermine paid Parks staff
- Member work complements and supports paid staff activities

**Support:**

- Training arranged or signposted where appropriate
- Members covered by FoLR insurance while conducting organizational work
- Policy reviewed annually

## Financial Rules & Management

**Governance:** Monthly general meetings serve as the management committee; members elect a Chair, Secretary, and Treasurer at the Annual General Meeting.

**Financial Records:** Maintained in computerized spreadsheets including all transactions, bank statements, cheques, fund receipts, and payment invoices. Financial year ends March 31st; annual accounts examined by an independent person and presented to the Annual General Meeting.

**Bank Account:**

- NatWest current account in name of Friends of Lordship
- Minimum three management committee signatories (including Treasurer)
- Two signatories required for withdrawals/transfers
- Monthly statements reviewed against account book

**Income:**

- Receipts issued for cash donations
- Two members count and sign for event cash
- All cash banked (£100+ within one week)
- Cheques recorded and banked within one month

**Expenditure:**

- All purchases agreed in advance at monthly meetings
- Supporting paperwork required for all payments
- Two signatures required on cheques (preferably including Treasurer)
- Non-cheque payments require signed payment forms with: payee, date, amount, payment type, and two signatures
- Blank cheques never signed

**Current Policy:** No petty cash system or staff wages currently in place; procedures outlined for potential future implementation.
