## Friends of Lordship Rec – Constitution

### Aims

The organization seeks to establish "a well-maintained and safe public park" benefiting the local community's physical and mental health. Key objectives include:

- Gathering perspectives from residents and park visitors
- Involving users in planning and management decisions
- Securing funding for facility improvements
- Encouraging community participation across all demographics
- Organizing social activities
- Protecting Haringey's green spaces

### Membership

Membership is available to anyone supporting the group's mission, regardless of background. Members must agree with stated aims and register their contact details. The group explicitly opposes "racism, sexism and homophobia."

### Meetings and Decision-Making

An Annual General Meeting occurs yearly to review activities, discuss finances, elect leadership (Secretary and Treasurer), and amend governance rules. At least two additional general meetings happen annually.

Decisions require consensus or simple majority voting, except constitutional amendments requiring two-thirds support at the AGM. Quorum is 4 members (6 at AGM).

### Operations

Working groups manage day-to-day activities including meeting planning, funding applications, stakeholder engagement with council and police, and financial management between general meetings.

### Finances and Dissolution

A dedicated bank account holds donations and subscriptions. Both Secretary and Treasurer must sign cheques. Independent annual verification is required.

If dissolved, assets transfer to a compatible voluntary organization per membership decision.

*Adopted October 5, 2003; amended December 5, 2004*
